Self-improvement, productivity, money, habits, leadership — this is the native language of social media. A product people already talk about

Nobody has to "review" Blinkist — they live it. The 15-minute Blink on a commute, the daily pick, the title that unlocked an idea. The product is the content: every Blink is a hook, every takeaway is a post.

02 · The Strategy

Don't bet on one creator size. Layer them.

The most common mistake is spending a whole budget on one big name — or only on cheap nano creators. Each tier does a different job. Combined, they cover the full funnel: reach at the top, conversion in the middle, authenticity and volume at the base. We build this same pyramid in each market, casting locally.

1

Cast across tiers

Ramped sources a mix of nano, micro and mid creators in the book, self-improvement, productivity and finance niches — native to each market.

2

Brief & create

Each creator runs a real Blinkist habit — a challenge, a playlist, a daily Blink — and produces authentic content, with paid usage rights included.

3

Concentrated launch

Creators post in the same window. The install spike lifts App Store ranking in Education / Books — extra organic installs on top.

4

Amplify winners

Top organic videos are boosted as paid ads. You spend media budget only on creative already proven to convert.

Why combine sizes (and not just go big)

A single big creator gives you one shot, one audience, one CPM. Ten layered creators give you ten audiences, ten content angles to test, and a portfolio that de-risks the campaign. If one underperforms, the budget moves — you're never all-in on a single bet.

Why layering drives more reach

Mid-tier creators create the awareness wave and social proof; micro creators deliver the best engagement and lowest cost per install; nano creators add authentic volume and niche reading communities. Audiences who see the same app from several creators they trust convert far better than from one big post.

05 · The Original Idea

The three markets aren't three campaigns. They're one engine.

Here's the move most agencies miss. Because CPMs fall as you go from Germany to Mexico to Argentina, every dollar buys 2× the reach in Mexico and 4× in Argentina. So we use the cheap markets as a live creative lab — and feed the winners upstream to where each install is worth the most.

🧪 Test cheap in Argentina

At a $10 CPM, $10k buys ~1,000,000 views. That's the cheapest place on earth to find out which hook, which angle and which creator format actually makes people install — fast, and on real audiences.

📈 Validate & scale in Mexico

The angles that win in Argentina get re-cast with Mexican creators at a $20 CPM. A huge, under-penetrated Spanish market — same language, proven creative, half the guesswork.

🏆 Deploy the winners in Germany

By the time budget hits the $40 CPM home market, you're not guessing. You're buying proven creative in front of your highest-LTV subscribers, where it pays back hardest.

The result: learning compounds across borders. Run alone, each market is a standalone buy. Run together, Argentina's cheap reach de-risks Mexico, and Mexico de-risks Germany. You pay the lowest price to learn and the highest price only for creative that's already proven to convert.
